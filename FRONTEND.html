Problem Context & Pipeline Architecture

ICU monitor alarm fatigue motivates this research prototype. The architecture below shows how the synthetic demo filters baseline wander, detects beats, reports signal quality, and illustrates noise-aware status decisions; it is not a clinical monitoring system.

1. Clean

Moving-Average Baseline Removal

Subtracts moving-average window bw (default 200 samples) from raw ECG. Preserves at least 85% of true 1.0 mV QRS amplitude while eliminating low-frequency wander.

2. Detect

Adaptive Detector + 200 ms Refractory

Differentiates and squares smoothed signal, applies moving window integration (mw), adaptive thresholding, and an explicit 200 ms refractory blanking period to prevent double-counting.

3. Judge

Fuzzy False Alarm Suppression

Evaluates trapezoidal membership sets on HR, CV, and SQI. When noise is detected without confident rhythm pattern, the monitor transitions to "Hold for review" rather than sounding a false alarm.

4. Optimize

Genetic Algorithm with Elitism

Tunes bw, sm, th, mw on 3 noisy training records. Objective is F1 score penalized by R-peak timing jitter. Elitism guarantees monotonic fitness improvement across generations.

RR-Feature ECG Analyzer

Analyzes sample ECG traces with heuristic R-peak and RR-interval methods, reporting heart rate, HRV metrics, and a rule-based rhythm summary for educational use.
